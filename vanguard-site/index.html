Risk Management Framework

  • > Collateral Ratios: 150%+ for Senior tranches, dynamic liquidation auctions if ratios drop below 120%.
  • > Credit Reserves: 10% of outstanding credit allocated to insurance fund from trading fees.
  • > Reputation Slashing: Blacklist mechanism for defaulters affecting VGRD rank and on-chain credit score.
  • > Revenue Escrow: WBS underlying revenue streams held in 30-day escrow before distribution.
