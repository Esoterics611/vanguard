Vanguard solves the biggest problem in Web3: getting anonymous internet strangers to actually ship things together. We built a military-inspired coordination system where contributors join squads, complete missions, climb ranks from Private to Captain, and share in bounty rewards. No doxxing. No politics. Just pure meritocracy — all verifiable on-chain. It's Discord meets Call of Duty meets a DAO that actually works.
